When will I get married?

What Vedic astrology actually looks at — and how to get the answer for your own chart.

The short answer

Marriage timing in Jyotish comes from the dasha of the 7th lord, Venus (or Jupiter for women), the Darakaraka or planets in the 7th house — triggered when transiting Jupiter or Saturn touches the 7th house or its lord. A chart usually shows two or three strong windows; the earliest one that has both a dasha and a transit trigger is the likeliest.

What decides it in your kundli

7th house and lord, Venus/Jupiter, Darakaraka, D9, Jupiter and Saturn transits. A generic horoscope cannot answer this, because every one of these depends on your exact ascendant, which changes every two hours.
