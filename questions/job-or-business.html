Should I do a job or business?

What Vedic astrology actually looks at — and how to get the answer for your own chart.

The short answer

Business is favoured by a strong 7th house (trade and partners), a strong 3rd and 11th (effort and gains), Mercury or Rahu well placed, and a 10th lord in the 7th, 3rd or 11th. A strong 6th house and Saturn favour service. Many charts support a job first and business in a later dasha.

What decides it in your kundli

7th, 10th, 6th, 3rd, 11th houses, Mercury, Saturn. A generic horoscope cannot answer this, because every one of these depends on your exact ascendant, which changes every two hours.
