    What a kundli shows

    A kundli (janam kundli or birth chart) is a map of the sky at the minute and place you were born. Vedic astrology reads it in the sidereal zodiac, which is fixed to the stars, so your signs here may differ by one from a Western horoscope.

    Four things in it matter most for a first reading:

    • Lagna (ascendant): the sign rising in the east at your birth. It sets the twelve houses and is the single most time-sensitive part of the chart; it changes about every two hours.
    • Moon sign (rashi): the sign the Moon was in. Indian horoscopes and rashifal are read from it.
    • Nakshatra: which of the 27 lunar mansions held the Moon. It sets your name syllable, your temperament and where your dasha sequence begins.
    • Dasha: the Vimshottari planetary period you are living through. Each mahadasha runs for years and is split into antardashas, which is why life has chapters.

    How this free kundli is calculated

    Planet positions come from the open astronomy-engine library and are checked against Swiss Ephemeris. We use Lahiri ayanamsha, whole-sign houses and the mean node for Rahu and Ketu, the most widely used conventions in India. The chart is the North Indian style: the top diamond is the 1st house and the numbers are the signs (1 is Aries, 12 is Pisces). Read the full method.

    Questions

    Is this kundli free?

    Yes. The chart, lagna, Moon sign, nakshatra and current dasha are free, with no sign-up. Your details are not sent anywhere.

    What if I do not know my exact birth time?

    Your Moon sign and nakshatra are usually right even if the time is off by an hour, but the lagna and houses can change. Use the closest time you have and treat the lagna as tentative.

    Why is my sign different from my Western zodiac sign?

    Vedic astrology uses the sidereal zodiac, which is about 24 degrees behind the tropical zodiac used in Western astrology, so most people's signs shift back by one.

    Which dasha system is used?

    Vimshottari dasha, the 120-year cycle counted from the Moon's nakshatra at birth, with years of 365.25 days.
