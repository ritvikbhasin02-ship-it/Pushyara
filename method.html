How we calculate your report

The calculation behind every Life Report

Every Pushyara Life Report is computed, not guessed. Here is exactly what runs behind it.

LayerWhat it does
Exact skyPlanet positions from the open astronomy-engine library, checked against Swiss Ephemeris on 200 random charts: every sign, nakshatra and lagna matched, with the largest difference about 14 arc-seconds.
Vedic conventionsSidereal zodiac with Lahiri ayanamsha, whole-sign houses, mean node for Rahu and Ketu.
Birth-time checkWe compute how many minutes your lagna stays the same either side of your time, and warn you when a planet or the Moon sits near an edge.
Dignity and yogasExaltation, own sign and moolatrikona by degree; Pancha Mahapurusha, Gajakesari, Budh-Aditya, Viparita Raja, Kendradhipati, and neecha bhanga with the rule that cancels it named.
JaiminiAtmakaraka (seven-karaka scheme) and Karakamsa from the navamsa.
Vimshottari dashaMahadasha and antardasha dates from the Moon's nakshatra, 365.25-day years.
TransitsSaturn, Jupiter and Rahu sign changes and stations over the next 12 months, counted from your Moon and lagna; Sade Sati and dhaiya.
This weekEach day graded from Chandra bala (the Moon's house from your natal Moon) and your tara, with Rahu Kaal from sunrise and sunset at your birth place.

What we do not claim

The astronomy is exact; the interpretation is classical tradition, written in plain language. Where the classical texts disagree, our lessons say so. Every timed statement goes into your prediction tracker, so you can judge the reading by what actually happens.
