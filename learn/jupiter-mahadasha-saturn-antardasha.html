Jupiter Mahadasha – Saturn Antardasha

About 2 years 6 months

Jupiter–Saturn lasts about 2 years 6 months. The Jupiter mahadasha sets the stage — growth, wisdom, wealth, children and ethics — and the Saturn sub-period brings in discipline, endurance, longevity, mass support, lasting success.

What it typically brings

  • From Jupiter: wisdom, wealth, children, ethics, protection, respect.
  • From Saturn: discipline, endurance, longevity, mass support, lasting success.
  • Watch: delays, depression, chronic pain, loneliness, poverty of spirit.

It depends on your chart

The results of Jupiter–Saturn depend on which houses Jupiter and Saturn rule and occupy for your ascendant, their strength, and the transits running at the time. Two people in the same sub-period can have opposite years.

What to do

Run a debt-and-deadline calendar; honour workers and keep promises exactly.
