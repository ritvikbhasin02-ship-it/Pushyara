Punarvasu Nakshatra

Deity: Aditi (mother of the gods) · Ruler: Jupiter · Gana: Deva

Punarvasu nakshatra, ruled by Jupiter and presided over by Aditi, is the return of light: optimistic, generous and resilient people who get second chances and give them.

What Punarvasu nakshatra means

Punarvasu means “return of the good” or “wealth again” (punar, again; vasu, good or wealth). It spans the end of Gemini and the start of Cancer.

Its deity is Aditi, the boundless mother of the Adityas, whose name means freedom from limits. The symbol is a quiver of arrows: many chances waiting to be fired. Valmiki's Ramayana places Rama's birth in this nakshatra, which is why it is associated with restored order and homecoming. Jupiter, its ruling planet, supports the theme of faith, teaching and restoration, and gives the nakshatra its warm, generous tone.

Personality

At their best, Punarvasu natives are kind, optimistic and philosophical. They recover from loss, forgive readily and are happiest when guiding or sheltering others. Jupiter as ruler gives wisdom, faith and a simple, generous nature. They are good at starting again, and they often cheer others up with the plain conviction that things will turn out all right.

In real life: personality

For example, after a failed tuition centre, Gopal, 41, in Madurai simply says 'Chalo, phir se' and restarts. Neighbours drop by for his cheerful advice, and he sometimes lends money too trustingly.

The shadow is over-trusting and repeating the same lesson. Good faith can be taken advantage of, and plans may circle back again before they move forward. Punarvasu grows by learning from the first round.

Career and money

Punarvasu suits teaching and mentoring, counselling, publishing and writing, philosophy and spiritual work, hospitality, travel and tourism, and work with children or families. Retail, trade and any role that welcomes people home also fit. Money tends to arrive in cycles, with recoveries after setbacks rather than a single steady climb. Roles with a sense of mission or guidance, rather than pure competition, suit it best.

In real life: work and money

Think of Kavita, a primary teacher in Kochi who left a school job, tutored from home, and later opened a small learning centre. Her income came in cycles, with a recovery each time.

Love and marriage

Punarvasu is kind, forgiving and loyal in love. It values home, friendship and shared ideals, and is quick to make up after quarrels. It may stay too long in a relationship out of hope. It makes a warm, supportive spouse and a gentle parent who builds a welcoming home.

In real life: love

Like Dev, who forgave his girlfriend within an hour of a quarrel and made tea for both. His hope is warm, and he may stay in a relationship longer than his friends would advise.

In Ashtakoota, Punarvasu is Deva gana with the cat as yoni, whose traditional enemy yoni is the rat. Deva pairs well with Deva and is generally acceptable with Manushya. The full chart outweighs any single factor.

Health

In the Kalapurusha scheme Gemini is the shoulders and arms and Cancer the chest, so Punarvasu is commonly linked with the shoulders and arms in padas 1 to 3, and the chest and lungs in pada 4. Lists differ on finer detail, and this is a symbolic map, not a diagnosis.

The four padas

Each pada is a quarter of the nakshatra, 3°20' wide, and takes its own navamsa sign, which shapes how the nakshatra shows up in a life.

Pada 1, Aries navamsa (Ke). Adventurous and active. Restless but optimistic, and quick to begin again after any setback.

Pada 2, Taurus navamsa (Ko). Steady and comfort-seeking. Builds values, resources and a secure home; the most practical pada.

Pada 3, Gemini navamsa (Ha). A communicator and teacher. Curious, expressive and fond of ideas, with the quickness of Gemini.

Pada 4, Cancer navamsa (Hi). Nurturing and home-loving. This pada falls in Cancer, ruled by the Moon, and shows the nakshatra at its most caring and protective.

Moon, lagna or a planet in Punarvasu

Moon in Punarvasu makes this your janma nakshatra, and the first mahadasha is Jupiter's, sixteen years long in full, less the part already run at birth. The mind is hopeful, adaptable and kind. Padas 1 to 3 give a Gemini Moon, pada 4 a Cancer Moon. After it, the mahadashas follow in their fixed order, next being Saturn.

Punarvasu lagna gives a gentle, friendly face and an open manner. Another planet in Punarvasu gains optimism and a capacity to recover, with Jupiter's generosity. Read it with its sign and house. In every case the pada, the sign and the house matter as much as the nakshatra.

Good activities on Punarvasu days

Muhurta Chintamani places Punarvasu in the chara (movable) class, together with Swati, Shravana, Dhanishta and Shatabhisha. It suits travel, buying a vehicle, moving house and other work that involves motion. Movable days are also used for pilgrimage, changing residence and starting work that needs mobility.

Questions

Is Punarvasu nakshatra good?

Yes. Punarvasu is a movable, benefic nakshatra associated with renewal, generosity and second chances. It is judged together with the Moon sign, the ascendant and the running dasha.

Which planet rules Punarvasu nakshatra?

Jupiter rules Punarvasu. A person born with the Moon here begins life in a Jupiter mahadasha, sixteen years long in full.

What are the Punarvasu nakshatra name letters?

The syllables are Ke, Ko, Ha and Hi for padas 1 to 4. A name traditionally begins with the syllable of the pada the Moon occupied at birth.

Which nakshatras are compatible with Punarvasu?

Compatibility is judged between two Moon nakshatras using Ashtakoota. Punarvasu is Deva gana, so other Deva nakshatras (Ashwini, Mrigashira, Pushya, Hasta, Swati, Anuradha, Shravana, Revati) score best on gana, and Manushya is usually acceptable. Its nadi is Adya, and a different nadi in the partner is preferred. No single factor decides a match.

Who is the deity of Punarvasu nakshatra?

Aditi, the boundless mother of the gods, presides over Punarvasu. The symbol is a quiver of arrows.
