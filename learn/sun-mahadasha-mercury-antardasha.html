Sun Mahadasha – Mercury Antardasha

About 0 years 10 months

Sun–Mercury lasts about 0 years 10 months. The Sun mahadasha sets the stage — visibility and authority — and the Mercury sub-period brings in intelligence, speech, business sense, writing, adaptability.

What it typically brings

  • From Sun: authority, confidence, health, government favour, father's support.
  • From Mercury: intelligence, speech, business sense, writing, adaptability.
  • Watch: nervousness, indecision, skin issues, poor communication.

It depends on your chart

The results of Sun–Mercury depend on which houses Sun and Mercury rule and occupy for your ascendant, their strength, and the transits running at the time. Two people in the same sub-period can have opposite years.

What to do

Write things down: weekly planning, clean accounts, one new skill per quarter.
