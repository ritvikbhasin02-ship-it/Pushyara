Hasta Nakshatra

Deity: Savitar (the sun's creative power) · Ruler: Moon · Gana: Deva

Hasta nakshatra is the star of skilled hands: clever, crafty, humorous and practical, turning ideas into real objects.

What Hasta nakshatra means

Hasta means “the hand”. It sits in Virgo, it is ruled by the Moon, and its deity is Savitar, the impelling solar power. In the Rig Veda Savitar is the golden-handed god who sets the world moving each dawn, and the Gayatri mantra is addressed to him. A hand that makes, heals and gives fits that picture well.

The symbol is an open hand, and the five stars of the nakshatra are traditionally seen as the palm and fingers. It stands for skill, dexterity, the power to hold, give and shape, and the ability to get things done with one's own effort.

Hasta falls in Virgo, the sign of service and skill, and the Moon's lordship makes the hand not only a tool but also a sensitive one. That is why Hasta is linked equally with craftspeople, healers and entertainers: all of them work with their hands and read what people need.

Personality

Strengths are skill, wit, practical intelligence and a lively sense of humour. Hasta natives learn fast, adapt easily and are good at fixing, making and organising. They are helpful and often charming, with the Moon giving them an easy way with people.

The shadow is a restless mind. The same cleverness can stay on the surface, jump from one idea to the next, or slide into shrewd shortcuts. The growth task is depth: stay with one craft long enough to master it.

In daily life this shows as someone who is never idle. Hasta natives tinker, tidy, mend and improvise, and they often have a wry joke ready. They prefer concrete results to long discussions and feel uneasy when their hands have nothing to do.

Career and money

Hasta suits anything done with the hands or with sharp practical intelligence: crafts, design, tailoring, repair and technical work, healing and therapies, pharmacy, writing, accounting, trading and retail. Performing arts that rely on dexterity also fit. Money usually comes through skill and steady service, and trade comes naturally.

Love and marriage

In love Hasta is caring, playful and practical. It shows affection by doing things for a partner, and it likes humour and an easy home life.

For matching, the Ashtakoota method looks at gana and yoni among other points. Hasta is Deva gana, and same-gana pairs score best. Its yoni is the female buffalo, shared with Swati, and the classical enemy of the buffalo is the horse. Nadi is Adi, and traditional matching prefers partners with a different nadi. These are only starting points.

Health

Classical lists link Virgo with digestion and the intestines, and Hasta is naturally tied to the hands and fingers. Texts differ on details, so read this as symbolism, not a diagnosis.

The core of the nakshatra stays the same in all four padas. The navamsa sign and its lord change the flavour.

Pada 1, Aries navamsa (Pu). Mars gives this pada speed and drive. It is the most energetic and entrepreneurial quarter, quick to start and to act.

Pada 2, Taurus navamsa (Sha). Venus brings taste and patience. This pada joins skill with beauty and a feel for value, and suits makers and designers.

Pada 3, Gemini navamsa (Na). Mercury sharpens the mind. This pada favours writing, speaking, trading and clever problem-solving.

Pada 4, Cancer navamsa (Tha). The Moon softens it. This pada is caring and protective, and leans toward healing, nursing and looking after others.

Moon, lagna or a planet in Hasta

Moon in Hasta. This is your janma nakshatra, and the Moon is in its own nakshatra here. The Vimshottari sequence begins with the Moon, whose mahadasha lasts 10 years in all; how much remains at birth depends on the Moon's degree. Such a person feels settled when their hands are busy and their mind has a task.

Hasta rising. A Virgo ascendant in Hasta gives a neat, quick, observant person with a helpful manner. Check the Moon and Mercury, since the nakshatra lord and the sign lord both matter.

Other planets in Hasta. A planet here picks up themes of skill, craft, service and practical results. Moon-ruled energy makes it more adaptable and people-aware.

Good activities on Hasta days

Hasta is a kshipra (light, swift) nakshatra in muhurta lists. It suits quick-result work: opening a shop, selling, starting a course of study, craft and fine arts, and making or wearing ornaments. Many muhurta lists also count Hasta among the nakshatras for marriage. It is a weaker choice for heavy, permanent undertakings. Practical use: these days suit starting something that should show results soon, such as a shop, a class, a craft or a sale. Choose another day for a long, slow build.

Questions

Is Hasta nakshatra good?

Hasta is regarded as a bright, helpful nakshatra, good for skill, trade and service. The thing to watch is a restless mind. The whole chart decides.

Which planet rules Hasta nakshatra?

The Moon rules Hasta, so life begins in the Moon mahadasha, which runs 10 years in all. The sign Virgo is ruled by Mercury.

What are the Hasta nakshatra name letters?

The syllables are Pu, Sha, Na and Tha for padas 1 to 4.

Which nakshatras are compatible with Hasta?

In Ashtakoota, Deva gana partners score best with Hasta, and Swati shares its buffalo yoni. Real compatibility needs both full charts.

What is the symbol of Hasta nakshatra?

The symbol is an open hand, standing for skill, dexterity and the power to make, hold and give.
