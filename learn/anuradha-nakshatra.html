Anuradha Nakshatra

Deity: Mitra (god of friendship) · Ruler: Saturn · Gana: Deva

Anuradha nakshatra is the star of the lotus and of friendship: devoted, organised and successful through alliances, often far from home.

What Anuradha nakshatra means

Anuradha is often glossed as “following Radha”, meaning the star after Vishakha, and also as “success” or “devotion”; sources vary. It sits wholly in Scorpio, it is ruled by Saturn, and its deity is Mitra, the Aditya of friendship, agreements and the bond that holds a group together.

The symbol is a lotus, which grows from mud and opens clean above the water. It stands for beauty and devotion that rise out of difficulty, and for loyalty that stays steady through hardship. Saturn adds patience and an ability to endure.

Mitra and Varuna are often named together in the Vedas, Mitra as the lord of day, light and agreement and Varuna as the lord of night and oaths. Mitra is the friendly side of that pair, which is why Anuradha is associated with kindness, trust and the keeping of one's word.

Personality

Strengths are loyalty, organisational skill, devotion and the ability to bring different people into one team. Anuradha natives often succeed through cooperation and are good at building groups, clubs and networks. They work hard, keep their word and carry a deep emotional world under a composed face.

The shadow is melancholy and control. They can brood, hold onto old hurts, or try to manage the people they love too tightly. The growth task is to trust friends without testing them.

In daily life this shows as a person who remembers every friend, builds communities and keeps showing up. They are patient with long processes, quiet about their feelings and slow to forgive a betrayal. They are happiest when they belong to a group they believe in.

Career and money

Anuradha suits management, organisations and associations, international or foreign-linked work, research, counselling, psychology, occult and healing studies, and any field built on teams and networks. Many do well away from their birthplace. Money tends to come steadily and through group effort.

Love and marriage

In love Anuradha is deeply devoted, and friendship comes first. It takes time to trust, then gives itself fully, and it values loyalty above show.

For matching, the Ashtakoota method looks at gana and yoni among other points. Anuradha is Deva gana, and same-gana pairs score best. Its yoni is the female deer, shared with Jyeshtha, and the classical enemy of the deer is the dog. Nadi is Madhya, and traditional matching prefers partners with a different nadi. These are only starting points.

Health

Classical lists link Scorpio with the reproductive and excretory organs, and some lists give Anuradha the lower abdomen. Sources differ, so read this as symbolism, not a diagnosis.

The core of the nakshatra stays the same in all four padas. The navamsa sign and its lord change the flavour.

Pada 1, Leo navamsa (Na). The Sun lends this pada confidence and leadership. It is the most visible quarter, good at heading a group.

Pada 2, Virgo navamsa (Ni). Mercury makes this pada practical and detail-minded. It suits administration, analysis and careful service.

Pada 3, Libra navamsa (Nu). Venus adds charm and balance. This pada is the most sociable and diplomatic, skilled at partnership.

Pada 4, Scorpio navamsa (Ne). Mars deepens everything. This pada is the most intense and private, drawn to research and hidden things.

Moon, lagna or a planet in Anuradha

Moon in Anuradha. This is your janma nakshatra. The Vimshottari sequence begins with Saturn, whose mahadasha lasts 19 years in all; how much remains at birth depends on the Moon's degree. The Moon is in Scorpio, its sign of debilitation, so such a person tends to feel deeply and needs secure, loyal bonds.

Anuradha rising. A Scorpio ascendant in Anuradha gives a composed, watchful, magnetic personality. Check Saturn and Mars, since the nakshatra lord and the sign lord both matter.

Other planets in Anuradha. A planet here picks up themes of friendship, teamwork, loyalty and perseverance. Saturn adds patience and endurance to whatever the planet does.

Good activities on Anuradha days

Anuradha is a mridu (soft, gentle) nakshatra in muhurta lists. It suits friendship, music and the arts, clothes and ornaments, and agreements built on goodwill. Many muhurta lists also count Anuradha among the nakshatras for marriage. It is a weaker choice for harsh or aggressive tasks. Practical use: these days suit making new friends, starting a partnership, forming an association, and gentle creative or musical activity. Choose another day for a confrontation.

Questions

Is Anuradha nakshatra good?

Anuradha is regarded as a steady, devoted nakshatra, strong for friendship, teamwork and perseverance. The thing to watch is brooding and control. The whole chart decides.

Which planet rules Anuradha nakshatra?

Saturn rules Anuradha, so life begins in the Saturn mahadasha, which runs 19 years in all. The sign Scorpio is ruled by Mars.

What are the Anuradha nakshatra name letters?

The syllables are Na, Ni, Nu and Ne for padas 1 to 4.

Which nakshatras are compatible with Anuradha?

In Ashtakoota, Deva gana partners score best with Anuradha, and Jyeshtha shares its deer yoni. Real compatibility needs both full charts.

What is the symbol of Anuradha nakshatra?

The symbol is a lotus, standing for beauty and devotion that rise out of difficulty.
