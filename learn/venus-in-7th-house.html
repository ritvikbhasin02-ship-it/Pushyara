Venus in the 7th house: Attractive, loving spouse; partnerships and luxury business favoured.

What it means

Venus — Shukracharya, guru of desire — carries love, beauty, comfort, vehicles, art, marriage, luxury. The 7th house (Kalatra Bhava) governs marriage, spouse, business partners, contracts, the public. Placed here, Venus's themes are expressed through this area of life: attractive, loving spouse; partnerships and luxury business favoured.

In career, love, money and health

  • Career: arts, fashion, luxury, hospitality, fragrance, film, design — coloured by the 7th house themes of marriage.
  • Relationships: directly affects partnership and marriage.
  • Money: money follows effort in this area.
  • Health: watch kidneys, hormones, reproductive system.

Houses it aspects from here

From the 7th house, Venus aspects your 1st house — self.

When it activates

The Venus mahadasha (20 years) and Venus sub-periods in other dashas bring this placement to the front. Transits of Jupiter and Saturn over this house trigger its events.
