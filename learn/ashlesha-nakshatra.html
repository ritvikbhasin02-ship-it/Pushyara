Ashlesha Nakshatra

Deity: Nagas (serpent deities) · Ruler: Mercury · Gana: Rakshasa

Ashlesha nakshatra, ruled by Mercury and presided over by the Nagas, produces strategic, perceptive and hypnotic people who read others instantly and hold hidden power.

What Ashlesha nakshatra means

Ashlesha means “the clinging” or “the embrace” (from shlish, to cling). It lies at the end of Cancer, just before the sign changes to Leo.

Its deities are the Nagas, the serpent beings of Vedic and Puranic lore, guardians of hidden treasure and of deep, unseen power. The symbol is the coiled serpent: patience, stored energy and the capacity to strike or to heal. Many teachers also link the serpent with kundalini. Mercury, its ruling planet, supplies speech and calculation, so the clinging here is as much mental as emotional.

Personality

At their best, Ashlesha natives are perceptive, strategic and magnetic. They notice what others hide, keep their own counsel and are deeply protective of the few they trust. Mercury gives a sharp mind, wit and skill with words. They are rarely fooled for long, and they choose their words and their timing with care.

The shadow is manipulation and suspicion. The same insight can turn into over-reading people, holding on to hurt or controlling through silence. Ashlesha grows by trusting some of what it sees and letting go of the rest.

Career and money

Ashlesha suits work that reads people and hidden patterns: psychology and counselling, politics and negotiation, investigation and intelligence, pharmacy and medicine, research, the occult sciences and strategic business. Money tends to come through knowledge and discreet influence, and often grows through timing rather than visible effort. It does best where discretion and timing matter more than visibility.

Love and marriage

Ashlesha is intense and private in love. Trust comes slowly but is total once given, and it can be possessive or guarded. It needs a partner who is honest and who does not take its silences personally. Once it feels safe, it becomes a deeply loyal and attentive partner.

In Ashtakoota, Ashlesha is Rakshasa gana with the cat as yoni, whose traditional enemy yoni is the rat. Rakshasa here describes a strong, earthy, forceful temperament, not anything demonic. The full chart outweighs any single factor.

Health

In the Kalapurusha scheme Cancer is the chest and stomach, so Ashlesha is commonly linked with the chest, stomach and digestive tract. Lists differ on finer detail, and this is a symbolic map, not a diagnosis.

The four padas

Each pada is a quarter of the nakshatra, 3°20' wide, and takes its own navamsa sign, which shapes how the nakshatra shows up in a life.

Pada 1, Sagittarius navamsa (Di). Philosophical and expressive. Wants meaning behind its strategy and often teaches or advises.

Pada 2, Capricorn navamsa (Du). Ambitious, cautious and disciplined. Plays the long game; the most guarded pada.

Pada 3, Aquarius navamsa (De). Detached, inventive and unconventional. Strong in research, technology and ideas.

Pada 4, Pisces navamsa (Do). Intuitive and emotional. The most psychic and compassionate pada, and the one at the gandanta junction.

Moon, lagna or a planet in Ashlesha

Moon in Ashlesha makes this your janma nakshatra, and the first mahadasha is Mercury's, seventeen years long in full, less the part already run at birth. The mind is quick, watchful and emotionally guarded. Pada 4 lies in the gandanta junction with Magha, and Ashlesha is counted among the six gandamoola nakshatras, for which some families keep a traditional shanti. It is a custom, not a verdict. After it, the mahadashas follow in their fixed order, next being Ketu.

Ashlesha lagna gives a penetrating gaze and a reserved manner. Another planet in Ashlesha becomes subtle, strategic and secretive, coloured by Mercury. Read it with its sign and house. In every case the pada, the sign and the house matter as much as the nakshatra.

Good activities on Ashlesha days

Muhurta Chintamani places Ashlesha in the tikshna (sharp or dreadful) class, together with Ardra, Jyeshtha and Mula. It suits strategic or technical work, preparing medicines and sadhana, and is usually avoided for gentle beginnings such as weddings. Classical texts connect it with tasks that need secrecy, strategy or a firm stand, and advise against it for gentle ceremonies.

Questions

Is Ashlesha nakshatra good?

Ashlesha is a sharp nakshatra with great depth, insight and strategic skill, though it asks for self-awareness around trust and control. It is judged together with the Moon sign, the ascendant and the running dasha.

Which planet rules Ashlesha nakshatra?

Mercury rules Ashlesha, while its deities are the Nagas. A person born with the Moon here begins life in a Mercury mahadasha, seventeen years long in full.

What are the Ashlesha nakshatra name letters?

The syllables are Di, Du, De and Do for padas 1 to 4. A name traditionally begins with the syllable of the pada the Moon occupied at birth.

Which nakshatras are compatible with Ashlesha?

Compatibility is judged between two Moon nakshatras using Ashtakoota. Ashlesha is Rakshasa gana, so other Rakshasa nakshatras score best on gana. Its nadi is Antya, and a different nadi in the partner is preferred. No single factor decides a match.

Who is the deity of Ashlesha nakshatra?

The Nagas, the serpent deities, preside over Ashlesha. The symbol is a coiled serpent.
