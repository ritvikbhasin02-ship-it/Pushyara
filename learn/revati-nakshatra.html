Revati Nakshatra

Deity: Pushan (nourisher, guide of journeys) · Ruler: Mercury · Gana: Deva

Revati nakshatra, the last of the 27 and ruled by Mercury, belongs to gentle, wise guides who protect travellers and nourish others.

What Revati nakshatra means

Revati means wealthy or shining. It is the last of the 27 nakshatras and ends the zodiac at the close of Pisces, so it completes the cycle and sends the Moon on towards Ashwini. The symbol is a fish, also given as a drum, and the star group lies near Zeta Piscium.

The deity is Pushan, the Vedic god of roads, herds and journeys, the nourisher who guides travellers and, in the Rig Veda, the souls of the departed. Mercury rules the star and adds speech, wit and trade. Revati carries a quiet sense of finishing and of safe passage.

Personality

Revati natives are gentle, kind and intuitive. They protect the weak, have a good way with animals and children, and often guide others without being asked. They are imaginative and artistic, and others feel safe around them.

In real life: personality

For example, Rukmini, 38, in Mangaluru notices the new colleague eating alone and quietly makes space for her. She feeds the street dog daily and finds it hard to say no.

The shadow is over-sensitivity and escapism. They may drift into dreams, avoid hard decisions or be taken advantage of because they trust too easily. The lesson is to be as clear about their own needs as they are about everyone else's.

In daily life this star shows as a person who notices who is left out of the room and quietly makes space for them. They are often good at finding the way, in a trip, a project or a family problem, and others follow without knowing why. The practical task is to set kind but firm limits, so that giving does not drain them. Classical practice for the star is simple worship of its deity, Pushan, the guide of travellers, kept neutral and free of fear.

Career and money

Travel and hospitality, shipping and aviation, art, music and film, healing and nursing, animal care, spiritual guidance, charity, teaching, nutrition and dairy all fit. Mercury adds writing, accounts and trade.

In real life: work and money

Think of Joseph, a tour guide in Goa who leads travellers with such care that they return as friends. He earns well when his sister handles bookings and accounts.

Pushan guides travellers, so roles that help others reach a destination suit them, from tour guides and logistics to counsellors. They tend to do best where the work is kind and where a practical partner handles the business side.

A useful way to read this star in a chart is to look at how the person handles endings. Revati natives are often the ones who see a project, a job or a relationship through to its close and help others move on. Periods of Mercury and Jupiter tend to bring chances to guide, teach or travel, and a clear plan keeps these from drifting.

Love and marriage

Revati is tender and devoted and tends to idealise love. They give freely and want a gentle, loyal partner. Disappointment follows when the real person differs from the dream.

In real life: love

Like Nisha, who sends her husband a soft message before every long flight. She gives deeply and trusts easily, and benefits from a partner who gently guards her time.

In Ashtakoota matching Revati is Deva gana, elephant yoni and Antya nadi. Deva with Deva or Manushya usually scores well on gana, a partner of a different nadi avoids nadi dosha, and the classical opposite of elephant yoni is lion yoni. Full matching needs both charts.

Health

Pisces governs the feet, and Revati is commonly linked with the feet and ankles. This is symbolism, not a medical statement.

Pada 1 (De), Sagittarius navamsa, ruled by Jupiter: wise, philosophical and generous, a natural teacher.

Pada 2 (Do), Capricorn navamsa, ruled by Saturn: practical, disciplined and more worldly in ambition.

Pada 3 (Cha), Aquarius navamsa, ruled by Saturn: humanitarian, original and somewhat detached.

Pada 4 (Chi), Pisces navamsa, ruled by Jupiter: the most mystical and compassionate pada. It is the last quarter of the zodiac and also a gandanta zone.

Moon, lagna or a planet in Revati

Moon in Revati (janma nakshatra): a kind, sensitive and imaginative mind. The first mahadasha is Mercury, which lasts 17 years in full, so early life centres on learning, siblings and friends. The balance at birth depends on the Moon's degree.

Revati is a gandamula nakshatra, and the last few degrees of Pisces form the gandanta junction with Aries. Tradition advises a calming rite at birth for the Moon here, and a good astrologer judges the exact degree before reading it strongly.

Lagna in Revati: a gentle, approachable bearing. Jupiter, lord of Pisces, and Mercury show how it is used.

Another planet in Revati: it reaches completion and gains a caring, guiding quality in the matters it rules.

Good activities on Revati days

Classical muhurta places Revati in the Mridu (soft) group with Mrigashira, Chitra and Anuradha. Soft days suit gentle beginnings: friendship, clothing and ornaments, music and the arts, and learning a craft. Revati also appears in the usual list of nakshatras accepted for marriage, though it falls in the panchaka period that some traditions avoid for roofing a house or travelling south.

Questions

Is Revati nakshatra good?

Revati is widely regarded as a gentle and fortunate star. It gives kindness, intuition and a guiding nature. Over-sensitivity is its test.

Which planet rules Revati nakshatra?

Mercury rules Revati. A person born with the Moon here begins life in a Mercury mahadasha, which is 17 years long in full.

What are the Revati nakshatra name letters?

The four padas give De, Do, Cha and Chi.

Which nakshatras are compatible with Revati?

Compatibility is judged by Ashtakoota between two Moon nakshatras, not by a fixed list. For Revati (Deva gana, Antya nadi, elephant yoni), Deva or Manushya partners usually score well on gana, a different nadi avoids nadi dosha, and the full charts must be checked as well.

Why is Revati the last nakshatra?

The zodiac is counted from Ashwini, so Revati falls at the end of Pisces and closes the cycle. It symbolises completion and a safe passage onwards.
