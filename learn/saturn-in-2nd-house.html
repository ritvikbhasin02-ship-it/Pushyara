Saturn in the 2nd house: Wealth comes slowly but stays; measured speech, family duties.

What it means

Saturn — Karma-phala data, the judge — carries discipline, endurance, longevity, mass support, lasting success. The 2nd house (Dhana Bhava) governs savings, family, speech, food habits, early values. Placed here, Saturn's themes are expressed through this area of life: wealth comes slowly but stays; measured speech, family duties.

In career, love, money and health

  • Career: management, labour, manufacturing, oil and mining, infrastructure, government service — coloured by the 2nd house themes of savings.
  • Relationships: influences family and speech.
  • Money: a direct wealth placement.
  • Health: watch joints, bones, teeth, chronic conditions.

Houses it aspects from here

From the 2nd house, Saturn aspects your 8th, 4th, 11th houses — transformation, home, gains.

When it activates

The Saturn mahadasha (19 years) and Saturn sub-periods in other dashas bring this placement to the front. Transits of Jupiter and Saturn over this house trigger its events.
