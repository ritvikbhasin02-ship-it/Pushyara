Krittika Nakshatra

Deity: Agni (fire) · Ruler: Sun · Gana: Rakshasa

Krittika nakshatra, ruled by the Sun and presided over by Agni, shapes sharp, purifying minds that cut through confusion and protect those they nurture.

What Krittika nakshatra means

Krittika comes from krit, to cut, and is called “the cutter”. It is the Pleiades, and it straddles two signs: the first pada falls in Aries and the other three in Taurus.

Its deity is Agni, the sacred fire that carries offerings to the gods and burns away what is impure. In the Puranas the six Krittikas are the nurses who raised Kartikeya, the war god, so the nakshatra joins cutting and nourishing. The symbol is a razor or flame: sharpness that cleans. The Sun rules it, which suits a nakshatra of fire, visibility and clear judgement.

Personality

At their best, Krittika natives are honest, disciplined and quietly authoritative. They see through pretence, defend those in their care and hold themselves to high standards. The Sun as ruler gives pride, clear purpose and a need to be taken seriously. They make good mentors because they say what is true, set clear standards and stay loyal to those who earn their respect.

In real life: personality

For example, Mr Verma, a school vice principal in Jaipur, spots a fudged attendance register in seconds. He says it plainly, and later wonders why the staffroom went quiet.

The shadow is harsh speech and a short temper. The blade that cuts through confusion also wounds people, and criticism can come out sharper than meant. Krittika grows by learning when not to cut.

Career and money

Krittika suits leadership and work that needs a clean cut: administration and government, the armed forces and police, surgery and dentistry, cooking, food businesses and anything involving fire or metal, editing, auditing and quality control. Teachers and mentors with high standards also fit. Money tends to come through authority and reputation for reliability, rather than through flexibility. Work with a clear standard, where quality can be seen and measured, brings out its best.

In real life: work and money

Think of Shabnam, a quality inspector at a Pune auto-parts plant, who rejects a whole batch over a small burr. Her reputation for being strict is why the owner trusts her signature.

Love and marriage

Krittika is warm and fiercely protective in love, but words can burn. It values honesty and loyalty and may be slow to show softness, which has to be learned. Its loyalty and sense of duty make it a dependable spouse, and its honesty is a strength once softened by kindness.

In real life: love

Like Vikram, who shows love by fixing his wife's scooter and packing her tiffin. Soft words come hard, so an honest 'I was wrong' arrives late, but it is meant.

In Ashtakoota, Krittika is Rakshasa gana with the sheep as yoni, whose traditional enemy yoni is the monkey. Rakshasa here describes a strong, earthy, forceful temperament, not anything demonic, and it scores best with other Rakshasa nakshatras. The full chart outweighs any single factor.

Health

In the Kalapurusha scheme Aries is the head and Taurus the face and throat, so Krittika is linked with the head, eyes, face and neck. Lists differ on finer detail, and this is a symbolic map, not a diagnosis.

The four padas

Each pada is a quarter of the nakshatra, 3°20' wide, and takes its own navamsa sign, which shapes how the nakshatra shows up in a life.

Pada 1, Sagittarius navamsa (A). The only pada in Aries, so the most fiery. Idealistic, principled and outspoken, with Jupiter's taste for truth and teaching.

Pada 2, Capricorn navamsa (I). The first pada in Taurus. Practical, ambitious and persistent; builds slowly and well, with Saturn's discipline and a love of food and comfort.

Pada 3, Aquarius navamsa (U). Detached and original. Likes systems, technology and causes, and can seem cool or private even when it cares deeply.

Pada 4, Pisces navamsa (E). Compassionate and intuitive. The gentlest pada, with a protective streak and a pull toward service, healing or spiritual life.

Moon, lagna or a planet in Krittika

Moon in Krittika makes this your janma nakshatra, and the first mahadasha is the Sun's, six years long in full, less the part already run at birth. The mind is sharp, proud and quick to judge. Pada 1 gives an Aries Moon, padas 2 to 4 a Taurus Moon. After it, the mahadashas follow in their fixed order, next being the Moon.

Krittika lagna gives a firm, clear gaze and a commanding manner. Another planet in Krittika becomes sharper, more critical and more purposeful, coloured by the Sun's authority. Read it with its sign and house. In every case the pada, the sign and the house matter as much as the nakshatra.

Good activities on Krittika days

Muhurta Chintamani places Krittika in the mishra (mixed) class, together with Vishakha. It is used for fire-related work such as cooking, metal work and preparing medicines, and for activities that need both force and care. It is mainly used for practical, fire-related tasks rather than for gentle ceremonies.

Questions

Is Krittika nakshatra good?

Krittika is a mixed nakshatra. It gives courage, honesty and purifying energy, but its sharpness has to be handled with care. It is judged together with the Moon sign, the ascendant and the running dasha.

Which planet rules Krittika nakshatra?

The Sun rules Krittika. A person born with the Moon here begins life in a Sun mahadasha, six years long in full.

What are the Krittika nakshatra name letters?

The syllables are A, I, U and E for padas 1 to 4. A name traditionally begins with the syllable of the pada the Moon occupied at birth.

Which nakshatras are compatible with Krittika?

Compatibility is judged between two Moon nakshatras using Ashtakoota. Krittika is Rakshasa gana, so other Rakshasa nakshatras score best on gana. Its nadi is Antya, and a different nadi in the partner is preferred. No single factor decides a match.

Who is the deity of Krittika nakshatra?

Agni, the sacred fire, presides over Krittika. The symbol is a razor or flame.
