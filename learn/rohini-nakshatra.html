Rohini Nakshatra

Deity: Brahma (the creator) · Ruler: Moon · Gana: Manushya

Rohini nakshatra, ruled by the Moon and presided over by Brahma, is magnetic, creative and grounded, the star of beauty, abundance and the power to make things grow.

What Rohini nakshatra means

Rohini means “the red one” or “the ascending, growing one”. It lies wholly in Taurus, the sign where the Moon is exalted, and the Moon rules it.

In the Puranic story Rohini is the favourite of Chandra's twenty-seven wives, the daughters of Daksha. Its deity is Brahma, also called Prajapati, the creator. The symbol is a chariot or ox-cart: fertility, wealth carried forward and steady progress. The Moon rules it, so Rohini carries the Moon's qualities of nourishment, receptivity and gentle growth.

Personality

At their best, Rohini natives are attractive, creative and calm in manner, with a gift for making things flourish, whether a garden, a business or a household. They enjoy beauty, music and good food and are generous with those they love. They are persuasive without effort, with a charm that draws people in, and they usually know what they want and quietly work to keep it.

In real life: personality

For example, Sunita, 45, in Nashik keeps a tulsi, a rose bed and a full kitchen going all year. Guests leave fed and happy, and she feels unsettled when the furniture is rearranged.

The shadow is attachment to comfort and to people. Rohini can cling, turn possessive or resist change, and can be moody when what it has built is threatened. It grows by loosening its grip.

Career and money

Rohini suits fields that create and sustain: agriculture and food, real estate and building, fashion, beauty and luxury goods, music and the arts, hospitality and retail. Moon-ruled work such as dairy, hotels and care also fits. Money tends to build steadily; this is a fixed nakshatra and rewards patience and ownership more than speed. A hands-on approach, where something visibly grows, suits Rohini better than abstract work.

In real life: work and money

Think of Naveen, who grew a small rice-and-spice shop in Guntur into three outlets over twelve years. He prefers owning a visible thing that grows to chasing quick trades.

Love and marriage

Love is a central theme for Rohini. It is romantic, affectionate and sensual, and can also be possessive. Home, family and beauty matter, and it usually wants a settled, comfortable partnership. Generosity, gifts and shared comforts are its love language, and it creates a warm, beautiful home.

In real life: love

Like Pooja, who plans anniversaries down to the flowers and the playlist. Gifts and a warm home are how she says love, and she gets quietly possessive when her partner makes plans without her.

In Ashtakoota, Rohini is Manushya gana with the serpent as yoni, whose traditional enemy yoni is the mongoose. Manushya pairs well with Manushya and is generally acceptable with Deva. Rohini is among the nakshatras classically favoured for marriage.

Health

In the Kalapurusha scheme Taurus is the face and throat, so Rohini is commonly linked with the face, mouth, throat and neck. Lists differ on finer detail, and this is a symbolic map, not a diagnosis.

The four padas

Each pada is a quarter of the nakshatra, 3°20' wide, and takes its own navamsa sign, which shapes how the nakshatra shows up in a life.

Pada 1, Aries navamsa (O). Energetic, ambitious and direct. The most active pada, with a Mars-like drive that pushes Rohini's creativity into enterprise.

Pada 2, Taurus navamsa (Va). The purest Rohini, with Taurus doubled: sensual, stable, artistic and money-minded, with great staying power.

Pada 3, Gemini navamsa (Vi). Curious, witty and talkative. Good with trade, media and ideas, and less rooted than the other three.

Pada 4, Cancer navamsa (Vu). Emotional, nurturing and home-loving. The most sensitive pada, with the Moon doubled and a strong bond to family and memory.

Moon, lagna or a planet in Rohini

Moon in Rohini makes this your janma nakshatra, and the first mahadasha is the Moon's, ten years long in full, less the part already run at birth. The mind is receptive, imaginative and drawn to comfort and beauty. After it, the mahadashas follow in their fixed order, next being Mars.

Rohini lagna gives an attractive, pleasing presence. Another planet in Rohini gains richness and staying power and may become possessive of what it values. Read it with its sign and house. In every case the pada, the sign and the house matter as much as the nakshatra.

Good activities on Rohini days

Muhurta Chintamani places Rohini in the dhruva (fixed or sthira) class. It suits lasting undertakings: laying a foundation, building or entering a house, planting, and starting a long-term job. It is also considered favourable for weddings. Its stable quality also makes it a favoured day for planting a garden and for any decision meant to last.

Questions

Is Rohini nakshatra good?

Yes. Rohini is a fixed, nourishing nakshatra associated with beauty, growth and stability, and is classically favoured for lasting work. It is judged together with the Moon sign, the ascendant and the running dasha.

Which planet rules Rohini nakshatra?

The Moon rules Rohini. A person born with the Moon here begins life in a Moon mahadasha, ten years long in full.

What are the Rohini nakshatra name letters?

The syllables are O, Va, Vi and Vu for padas 1 to 4. A name traditionally begins with the syllable of the pada the Moon occupied at birth.

Which nakshatras are compatible with Rohini?

Compatibility is judged between two Moon nakshatras using Ashtakoota. Rohini is Manushya gana, so other Manushya nakshatras score best on gana, and Deva is usually acceptable. Its nadi is Antya, and a different nadi in the partner is preferred. No single factor decides a match.

Who is the deity of Rohini nakshatra?

Brahma, the creator also called Prajapati, presides over Rohini. The symbol is a chariot or ox-cart.
