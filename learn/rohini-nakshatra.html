Rohini Nakshatra

Deity: Brahma (the creator) · Ruler: Moon · Gana: Manushya

Magnetic, creative and grounded. The Moon's favourite star: beauty, abundance and the power to grow things.

Rohini at a glance

Span10°00' Taurus – 23°19' Taurus
Ruling planetMoon (starts the Moon mahadasha at birth)
DeityBrahma (the creator)
SymbolChariot / ox-cart
GanaManushya
NatureFixed
Name syllables (pada 1–4)O · Va · Vi · Vu
SignTaurus

Career

Agriculture, real estate, luxury, fashion, music, hospitality, food.

Love & marriage

Romantic, affectionate and possessive; love is a central life theme.

The shadow

Attachment to comfort and to people.
