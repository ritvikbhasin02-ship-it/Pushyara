The 12 Houses (Bhavas)

Twelve houses, twelve areas of life.

1st House — Self

body, personality, health, how the world first sees you

2nd House — Wealth

savings, family, speech, food habits, early values

3rd House — Courage

effort, siblings, skills, media, short travel, self-made initiative

4th House — Home

mother, property, vehicles, inner peace, education base

5th House — Creativity

intelligence, children, romance, mantra, speculation, past-life merit

6th House — Service

competition, debts, disease, daily work, employees, litigation

7th House — Partnership

marriage, spouse, business partners, contracts, the public

8th House — Transformation

longevity, sudden events, secrets, research, inheritance, occult

9th House — Fortune

luck, father, guru, higher learning, long journeys, faith

10th House — Career

profession, status, authority, reputation, action in the world

11th House — Gains

income, networks, elder siblings, fulfilment of desires

12th House — Liberation

expenses, foreign lands, sleep, solitude, spirituality, moksha
