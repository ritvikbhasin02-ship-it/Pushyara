Mrigashira Nakshatra

Deity: Soma (the Moon nectar) · Ruler: Mars · Gana: Deva

Mrigashira nakshatra, ruled by Mars and presided over by Soma, belongs to gentle, curious seekers who keep searching for the perfect answer, place or partner.

What Mrigashira nakshatra means

Mrigashira means “the deer's head” (mriga, deer; shira, head), and is also read as “the searching one”. It spans the end of Taurus and the start of Gemini.

Its deity is Soma, the Moon nectar of the Vedas, the plant and essence that sustains life. Vedic sky-lore shows the deer pursued by a hunter, which ties this nakshatra to the act of seeking itself. The symbol is the deer's head: alertness, gentleness and a mind always scanning the horizon. Mars, its ruling planet, gives the deer a restless, forward-moving energy, so the search is active rather than passive.

Personality

At their best, Mrigashira natives are curious, gentle and quick to learn, with a talent for words, music and ideas. They are good listeners and make pleasant company, and Mars gives them a thread of determination under the softness. They tend to look younger than their years, to speak softly and to collect interests, places and friends the way others collect possessions.

The shadow is restlessness and indecision. The search for the perfect option can stop a decision from ever being made, and attention moves on before the work is done. Mrigashira grows by choosing and staying.

Career and money

Mrigashira suits work built on searching and exchange: research, writing and journalism, travel and tourism, sales and marketing, textiles and clothing, perfume and aesthetics, music and design. Mars adds a practical, sometimes technical, edge. Money tends to come through skill with people and information, and income may move across several fields before it settles. Roles that allow movement, new people and new information keep it engaged far longer than a fixed routine.

Love and marriage

Mrigashira is sweet, playful and a little flirtatious in love. It needs mental stimulation and variety of conversation to stay interested, and may be slow to commit while still looking. It does best with a partner who is also a friend and who enjoys conversation, music and travel.

In Ashtakoota, Mrigashira is Deva gana with the serpent as yoni, whose traditional enemy yoni is the mongoose. Deva pairs well with Deva and is generally acceptable with Manushya. Mrigashira is also among the nakshatras classically favoured for marriage.

Health

In the Kalapurusha scheme Taurus is the face and throat and Gemini the shoulders and arms, so Mrigashira is commonly linked with the face, chin, throat and shoulders. Lists differ on finer detail, and this is a symbolic map, not a diagnosis.

The four padas

Each pada is a quarter of the nakshatra, 3°20' wide, and takes its own navamsa sign, which shapes how the nakshatra shows up in a life.

Pada 1, Leo navamsa (Ve). Taurus, with a Leo flavour: confident and proud, a seeker who wants recognition for what it finds.

Pada 2, Virgo navamsa (Vo). Taurus, with a Virgo flavour: analytical and exact, excellent for research, craft and detailed writing.

Pada 3, Libra navamsa (Ka). Gemini, with a Libra flavour: sociable, artistic and partnership-minded, with a strong sense of beauty.

Pada 4, Scorpio navamsa (Ki). Gemini, with a Scorpio flavour: intense and investigative, secretive and driven to find what is hidden.

Moon, lagna or a planet in Mrigashira

Moon in Mrigashira makes this your janma nakshatra, and the first mahadasha is Mars, seven years long in full, less the part already run at birth. The mind is inquisitive, soft-spoken and restless. Padas 1 and 2 give a Taurus Moon, padas 3 and 4 a Gemini Moon. After it, the mahadashas follow in their fixed order, next being Rahu.

Mrigashira lagna gives a gentle, youthful look and an alert manner. Another planet in Mrigashira becomes more curious and mobile, a seeker by nature. Read it with its sign and house. In every case the pada, the sign and the house matter as much as the nakshatra.

Good activities on Mrigashira days

Muhurta Chintamani places Mrigashira in the mridu (soft or mild) class, together with Chitra, Anuradha and Revati. It suits singing, music, new clothes, making friends, ornaments and games, and is also favoured for weddings. It is a good day for learning music, buying clothes and starting friendships, or a trip with a light purpose.

Questions

Is Mrigashira nakshatra good?

Yes. It is a soft, gentle nakshatra suited to learning, art, friendship and travel. Its main challenge is restlessness. It is judged together with the Moon sign, the ascendant and the running dasha.

Which planet rules Mrigashira nakshatra?

Mars rules Mrigashira. A person born with the Moon here begins life in a Mars mahadasha, seven years long in full.

What are the Mrigashira nakshatra name letters?

The syllables are Ve, Vo, Ka and Ki for padas 1 to 4. A name traditionally begins with the syllable of the pada the Moon occupied at birth.

Which nakshatras are compatible with Mrigashira?

Compatibility is judged between two Moon nakshatras using Ashtakoota. Mrigashira is Deva gana, so other Deva nakshatras (Ashwini, Punarvasu, Pushya, Hasta, Swati, Anuradha, Shravana, Revati) score best on gana, and Manushya is usually acceptable. Its nadi is Madhya, and a different nadi in the partner is preferred. No single factor decides a match.

Who is the deity of Mrigashira nakshatra?

Soma, the Moon nectar of the Vedas, presides over Mrigashira. The symbol is a deer's head.
