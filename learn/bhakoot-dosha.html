Bhakoot dosha: 6/8, 5/9 and 2/12 explained

The 7-point koota that scores zero for half of all Moon sign pairs.

Bhakoot compares the distance between the two Moon signs. If the pair is 6/8, 5/9 or 2/12 it scores 0 out of 7, and otherwise 7. The dosha is considered cancelled when the two sign lords are friends or the same planet.
In real life

For example, Ritu and Sameer like each other, and the pandit says their Moon signs are 6/8 and writes zero for Bhakoot. Ritu’s father asks the first thing worth asking: are the two rashi lords friends?

What is Bhakoot?

Bhakoot (also spelled Bhakut) is the seventh koota in guna milan, worth 7 points. It looks only at the two Moon signs (rashis) and asks how far apart they are. Traditionally it describes the long arc of a shared life: money, health, children and whether the household keeps rising or keeps leaking energy.

Learn the basics: Moon sign and nakshatra

Kundli matching starts from the Moon, not the Sun. Your Moon sign (rashi) is the zodiac sign the Moon was in when you were born. Your nakshatra (birth star) is the one of 27 equal slices of the sky, each 13°20' wide, that the Moon was crossing, and each nakshatra has four quarters called padas. Vedic astrology treats the Moon as the mind, meaning how you feel, react and settle, which is why it carries the match. A koota is one of the eight scored categories, a dosha is a flagged imbalance in a chart, a dasha is a planetary period that runs for years, and the Navamsa (D9) is the nine-part chart classically used to read marriage. You can find your Moon sign and nakshatra in the free kundli.

How the distance is counted

Count from the boy’s Moon sign to the girl’s, with the first sign as 1, then count back the other way. Say the boy’s Moon is in Taurus and the girl’s in Virgo. Forward from Taurus: Taurus 1, Gemini 2, Cancer 3, Leo 4, Virgo 5. Back from Virgo to Taurus: Virgo 1, Libra 2, Scorpio 3, Sagittarius 4, Capricorn 5, Aquarius 6, Pisces 7, Aries 8, Taurus 9. The pair is 5/9. The two numbers always add up to 14, so you only need to count one way.

How common is it?

For any Moon sign, six of the twelve possible partner signs are flagged, two of each type. So half of all random pairs score 0 on Bhakoot, before any cancellation. That makes it far more common than most people assume, and it is worth remembering when a pandit announces it as if it were rare. Together with Nadi, the two heaviest kootas hold 15 of the 36 points.

When Bhakoot dosha is cancelled

Classical practice cancels the dosha in the following cases:

  • The two sign lords are friends. In the Taurus and Virgo example, the lords are Venus and Mercury, which are friends, so the 5/9 is treated as cancelled.
  • Both signs have the same lord. Aries and Scorpio are a 6/8 pair, but both are ruled by Mars, so the dosha does not stand.

When the lords are not friendly, for example Aries (Mars) and Virgo (Mercury), the dosha stands. The Pushyara tool keeps the koota score at 0 but writes the cancellation in the notes box, because most pandits apply it when they read the match and you should see both versions.

In real life: reading the numbers

For example, two cousins in a joint family in Kanpur each bring a Bhakoot zero to a family meeting. One pair has friendly lords and the other does not, so the same zero deserves two different conversations.

What it is really asking

Stripped of the vocabulary, Bhakoot is asking whether the two of you pull in the same direction on the large, slow things: how you save and spend, how you look after your health, and how you plan a family. The Moon signs are a crude proxy for that. A better way to check the same questions is to look at the 2nd, 4th, 5th and 11th houses and the dasha timings in both full charts, and, in plain life, to talk about money and family early.

What to do with a Bhakoot zero

  1. Count the distance yourself to confirm the pair.
  2. Check whether the sign lords are friends or the same planet.
  3. Look at the other seven kootas. Graha Maitri in particular tells you if the minds get along.
  4. If it still stands, ask for the full-chart reading, which gives actual dates for the money and family periods in each life.

Frequently asked questions

What is Bhakoot dosha?

It is a zero in the 7-point Bhakoot koota, given when the two Moon signs form a 6/8, 5/9 or 2/12 pair.

Is 6/8 always bad in kundli matching?

It is the pair most worried about, but it is cancelled in classical practice when the two sign lords are friends or the same planet. It is a prompt to look at the full charts and not a verdict.

Is 5/9 bad in kundli matching?

Bhakoot penalises it, mainly out of concern about children, even though a 5/9 relation is a friendly trine elsewhere in astrology. The friendly-lords cancellation applies here too.

How many couples have Bhakoot dosha?

Half of all random Moon sign pairs fall in a flagged relation, before cancellations are applied.

Can Bhakoot dosha be cancelled?

Yes, when the lords of the two Moon signs are friends or are the same planet. Some pandits apply further exceptions, and not all texts agree.
