Shravana Nakshatra

Deity: Vishnu (the preserver) · Ruler: Moon · Gana: Deva

Shravana nakshatra, ruled by the Moon and presided over by Vishnu, is the star of the listener who learns by hearing and connects people.

What Shravana nakshatra means

Shravana comes from the Sanskrit root shru, to hear. Its symbol is the ear, and the Vedas themselves are called shruti, that which is heard. The star lies in Capricorn and is made of three stars of the constellation Aquila.

The deity is Vishnu the preserver. The symbol is also given as three footprints, recalling Vishnu's three strides across the worlds as Vamana, which the Rig Veda already celebrates. The Moon rules the star and gives receptivity and memory. Shravana is a star of learning, carrying knowledge across generations and keeping society together.

Personality

Shravana natives are good listeners and keen observers. They learn more from people than from books, remember what they hear and pass it on with care. They are usually diplomatic, respectful of elders and tradition, and trusted with other people's confidences.

The shadow is sensitivity to criticism and gossip. They absorb what they hear, so a harsh word or a rumour can stay with them for a long time. They may also lean too heavily on other people's opinions before acting. Learning to filter what they take in is the lesson.

In daily life this star shows as a person who asks questions and then waits for the full answer. They are the ones who remember what was said at a meeting or at a family gathering, and who quietly hold a group together. The practical task is to choose carefully what they let in, whether news, gossip or advice, and to speak up for their own view as readily as they hear others. Classical practice for the star is simple worship of its deity, Vishnu, and regular listening to scripture, kept neutral and free of fear.

Career and money

Education and teaching, counselling, languages and translation, broadcasting and radio, journalism, libraries and archives, publishing, telecom and IT networking, travel and hospitality all fit. So do roles that connect people, such as human resources, mediation and public relations.

The Moon's rule favours work with the public. Income can rise and fall with cycles, so stored skills and steady employment are the real capital. Vishnu's preserving nature suits roles that maintain systems more than ones that break them.

Love and marriage

Shravana is attentive and caring, and communication is how they show love. They need to feel heard and will listen in return. They are loyal to family and tradition and look for a partner who talks things through.

In Ashtakoota matching Shravana is Deva gana, monkey yoni and Antya nadi. Deva with Deva or Manushya usually scores well on gana, a partner of a different nadi avoids nadi dosha, and the classical opposite of monkey yoni is sheep yoni. Full matching needs both charts.

Health

Shravana is classically linked with the ears and hearing. In the Kalapurusha scheme Capricorn governs the knees and bones. Treat this as symbolism, not a medical statement.

Pada 1 (Khi), Aries navamsa, ruled by Mars: a quick learner who speaks up and acts on what is heard. Impatience is the risk.

Pada 2 (Khu), Taurus navamsa, ruled by Venus: steady, musical and fond of comfort and good food. Values and security matter.

Pada 3 (Khe), Gemini navamsa, ruled by Mercury: talkative, curious and a natural storyteller or writer. The mind can be restless.

Pada 4 (Kho), Cancer navamsa, ruled by the Moon: nurturing, sentimental and attached to home and mother. This pada feels the most.

Moon, lagna or a planet in Shravana

Moon in Shravana (janma nakshatra): the Moon rules this star, so the mind is receptive and adaptable with a strong memory. Moods follow what is heard. The first mahadasha is the Moon, which lasts 10 years in full, so early life is tied to mother and home. The balance at birth depends on the Moon's degree.

Lagna in Shravana: an attentive, careful manner and a sense of duty. Saturn, lord of Capricorn, and the lagna lord decide the results.

Another planet in Shravana: it learns by listening and connects. It shows what the person absorbs from others in the matters that planet and its house rule.

Good activities on Shravana days

Classical muhurta places Shravana in the Chara (movable) group with Punarvasu, Swati, Dhanishta and Shatabhisha. Movable days suit travel, buying or using a vehicle, moving house and starting a course of study where listening matters. Vishnu-related observances are also often kept when the Moon is in Shravana.

Questions

Is Shravana nakshatra good?

Shravana is widely regarded as a fortunate star. It gives learning, good memory, reputation and the ability to connect people. Over-sensitivity to criticism is its test.

Which planet rules Shravana nakshatra?

The Moon rules Shravana. A person born with the Moon here begins life in a Moon mahadasha, which is 10 years long in full.

What are the Shravana nakshatra name letters?

By the Avakahada chakra used for Hindu naming, the four padas give Khi, Khu, Khe and Kho (खी, खू, खे, खो). Many families use the first syllable of the Moon’s pada for a baby’s name.

Which nakshatras are compatible with Shravana?

Compatibility is judged by Ashtakoota between two Moon nakshatras, not by a fixed list. For Shravana (Deva gana, Antya nadi, monkey yoni), Deva or Manushya partners usually score well on gana, a different nadi avoids nadi dosha, and the full charts must be checked as well.

What is the symbol of Shravana nakshatra?

An ear, also given as three footprints. The ear stands for listening and sacred knowledge, and the footprints for Vishnu's three strides.
