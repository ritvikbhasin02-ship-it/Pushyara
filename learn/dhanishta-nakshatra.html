Dhanishta Nakshatra

Deity: Eight Vasus (gods of abundance) · Ruler: Mars · Gana: Rakshasa

Dhanishta nakshatra, ruled by Mars and linked to the eight Vasus, is the star of rhythm, wealth and fame, where people prosper in groups.

What Dhanishta nakshatra means

Dhanishta, also called Shravishtha, is read as most wealthy or the swiftest. Its symbol is the drum, the beat that gathers a crowd and keeps it in time. The first two padas lie in Capricorn and the last two in Aquarius.

The deity is the eight Vasus, the gods of the natural elements and of abundance. In the Mahabharata the Vasus were cursed by the sage Vasishtha for stealing his cow. Seven were freed at once, and the eighth lived a long life on earth as Bhishma. The story ties this star to wealth, fame and the price of taking what is not yours. Mars rules it and gives energy and drive.

Personality

Dhanishta natives are energetic, ambitious and generous, with a feel for rhythm and timing. They like company, do well in teams and are often musical or athletic. Mars gives courage and the will to finish what they start.

The shadow is materialism and arrogance. They may talk too much about status, lose patience quickly, or put their own gain ahead of the group. Keeping the group's rhythm and not only their own is the lesson.

In daily life this star shows as a person who is happiest when something is moving: a project, a band, a match, a business. They often keep a beat, literally or in the rhythm of their day, and become restless when routine goes flat. The practical task is to channel Mars into a steady practice, such as training, a craft or regular saving, so that energy turns into lasting gain. Classical practice for the star is simple worship of its deities, the Vasus, kept neutral and free of fear.

Career and money

Music, dance and the performing arts, sports, real estate and construction, events and entertainment, banking and finance, technology and engineering, and security or military work all fit. Mars also suits surgery and any trade that needs speed and nerve.

Wealth is built into the name, and money tends to follow when they lead a team or belong to a strong one. They earn better as part of an orchestra than as a solo player. What this means in a given chart depends on the lords of the second and eleventh houses.

A useful way to read this star in a chart is to look at the company the person keeps. Dhanishta natives tend to rise with their circle, so the choice of partners, teammates and mentors shapes outcomes more than for most. Timing follows the same pattern: periods of Mars and of its lord's house often bring visible activity, movement and public notice.

Love and marriage

Dhanishta is generous but independent. They enjoy the chase and the company of friends, and marriage may need conscious effort to give a partner steady time. A partner who respects their drive and their social life suits them best.

In Ashtakoota matching Dhanishta is Rakshasa gana, lion yoni and Madhya nadi. Rakshasa partners score best on gana, a partner of a different nadi avoids nadi dosha, and the classical opposite of lion yoni is elephant yoni. Full matching looks at both charts.

Health

Capricorn governs the knees and Aquarius the calves and ankles. Lists differ on Dhanishta itself, some giving the back and waist and others the knees and calves. This is symbolic, not diagnostic.

Pada 1 (Ga), Leo navamsa, ruled by the Sun: charismatic and proud, a natural performer who wants to lead. Ego needs watching.

Pada 2 (Gi), Virgo navamsa, ruled by Mercury: skilled, technical and helpful. It combines drive with attention to detail.

Pada 3 (Gu), Libra navamsa, ruled by Venus: artistic, social and partnership-minded. Music and design come naturally.

Pada 4 (Ge), Scorpio navamsa, ruled by Mars: intense, secretive and resourceful. This is the pada with the most determination.

Moon, lagna or a planet in Dhanishta

Moon in Dhanishta (janma nakshatra): an energetic mind that responds to rhythm and company and dislikes idleness. The first mahadasha is Mars, which lasts 7 years in full. Early life can be active and competitive, with the balance at birth set by the Moon's degree.

Lagna in Dhanishta: an active, rhythmic bearing and a pull towards leading groups. The lagna lord and Mars show the quality of that leadership.

Another planet in Dhanishta: it gains drive and public visibility. Planets in the Capricorn half are more disciplined and those in the Aquarius half more social and unconventional.

Good activities on Dhanishta days

Classical muhurta places Dhanishta in the Chara (movable) group with Punarvasu, Swati, Shravana and Shatabhisha. Movable days suit travel, buying a vehicle, starting a performance or a trade, and moving house. The second half of Dhanishta begins the panchaka period, which many traditions avoid for roofing a house or travelling south.

Questions

Is Dhanishta nakshatra good?

Dhanishta is widely regarded as a fortunate star for wealth, fame and teamwork. Its tests are arrogance and putting status before people.

Which planet rules Dhanishta nakshatra?

Mars rules Dhanishta. A person born with the Moon here begins life in a Mars mahadasha, which is 7 years long in full.

What are the Dhanishta nakshatra name letters?

The four padas give Ga, Gi, Gu and Ge.

Which nakshatras are compatible with Dhanishta?

Compatibility is judged by Ashtakoota between two Moon nakshatras, not by a fixed list. For Dhanishta (Rakshasa gana, Madhya nadi, lion yoni), a Rakshasa partner scores best on gana, a different nadi avoids nadi dosha, and the full charts must be checked as well.

Which sign is Dhanishta nakshatra in?

The first two padas lie in Capricorn, from 23°20', and the last two in Aquarius, up to 6°40'.
