Mula Nakshatra

Deity: Nirriti (goddess of dissolution) · Ruler: Ketu · Gana: Rakshasa

Mula nakshatra, the root star of Sagittarius ruled by Ketu, belongs to people who dig to the bottom of everything and are asked by life to rebuild from the foundation.

What Mula nakshatra means

Mula means root. It points to the part of a plant that lies hidden underground, holds everything up and, when pulled out, ends the plant. The stars of Mula form a cluster at the tail of the Scorpion, and its symbol is a bundle of tied roots. Roots are not meant to be seen, yet they feed, anchor and finally decide what survives.

The deity is Nirriti, the goddess of dissolution and decay, named in the Rig Veda and the Atharva Veda as the power that loosens what has outlived its time. She is not evil. A field must be cleared before it is sown, and Nirriti is that clearing. Ketu, the planet of detachment, rules the star, and it sits at the start of Sagittarius, the sign of dharma and the search for truth.

Personality

Mula natives want the root cause. They question received ideas, read the fine print of life and are rarely satisfied with a surface answer. They are honest to a fault, take up research or hard subjects that others avoid, and often stay calm when a situation falls apart because they half expected it.

In real life: personality

For example, Vishal, 26, in Ranchi keeps asking 'but why?' at his first job until he finds the real cause of a fault. He then leaves for a research lab, to his parents' surprise.

The shadow is destructive restlessness. Because Mula tears down before it builds, these people may leave jobs, cities or relationships abruptly, burn bridges in a moment of frustration, or dig so deep into what is wrong that they miss what is right. The lesson is to uproot only what is truly dead and to rebuild with the same energy.

Career and money

Mula suits work that goes underneath: medical research, pharmacology and herbal medicine, diagnostics, philosophy and comparative religion, investigation, audit and forensic work, archaeology, geology and mining. Teaching spiritual or scientific subjects also fits, as does any role that dismantles an old system, such as restructuring or turnaround work.

In real life: work and money

Think of Dr Anita, a diagnostics lab scientist in Vellore who dug through years of reports to find an error nobody had checked. Her reputation comes from depth, not publicity.

Ketu favours expertise over show, so reputation is built by depth of knowledge and not by publicity. Many Mula natives break from the family trade or the path expected of them, and their work settles once they commit to one deep field.

Love and marriage

Mula loves with intensity and honesty. These people look for a partner who can face hard truths, and a relationship that stays on the surface will not hold them. Many go through one major break or change in love before they settle, and the partner often becomes a mirror that shows what must change.

In real life: love

Like Arman, who insists on honest talk about money and family before an engagement. A surface-level romance does not hold him, and he looks for a partner who can discuss hard truths.

In Ashtakoota matching Mula is Rakshasa gana, dog yoni and Adi nadi. Partners of the same gana score best on gana, and a partner whose nadi is not Adi avoids nadi dosha. The classical opposite of dog yoni is deer yoni. These are three of eight factors, and the two full charts matter more than any one of them.

Health

In the Kalapurusha scheme Sagittarius governs the thighs, and many lists give Mula the feet and thighs. Traditions differ on the exact assignment, so take this as symbolism and not as a diagnosis.

Pada 1 (Ye), Aries navamsa, ruled by Mars: the most forceful pada. It is direct, pioneering and quick to act, with courage and a hunger for independence. Impatience is the risk.

Pada 2 (Yo), Taurus navamsa, ruled by Venus: steadier and more practical. Comfort, security and good taste ground the Mula search, and stubbornness is the thing to watch.

Pada 3 (Bha), Gemini navamsa, ruled by Mercury: curious, talkative and analytical. Writing, teaching and research come naturally, and the mind can scatter over too many questions.

Pada 4 (Bhi), Cancer navamsa, ruled by the Moon: the most emotional pada. It is protective of family, intuitive and moody, and often searches for inner security and a sense of home.

Moon, lagna or a planet in Mula

Moon in Mula (janma nakshatra): the mind is searching, blunt and prone to sudden turns. The first mahadasha is Ketu, which lasts 7 years in full; the balance at birth depends on how far the Moon has moved through the star. Early years often bring changes of place or a feeling of being different from the family.

Mula is one of the six gandamula nakshatras, for which tradition advises a calming rite to Nirriti or the star's deity at birth. A good astrologer judges the pada and the Moon's degree before reading this too strongly.

Lagna in Mula: the temperament is direct and the person looks for meaning in what they do. Change of direction at least once in life is common, and the strength of the lagna lord and Jupiter, lord of Sagittarius, shows how well this energy is used.

Another planet in Mula: it works at depth and may bring a clearing followed by a stronger rebuild in the matters that planet and its house rule.

Good activities on Mula days

Classical muhurta calls Mula a Tikshna (sharp) nakshatra, grouped with Ardra, Ashlesha and Jyeshtha. Sharp days suit uprooting and breaking: demolition, clearing land, ending an arrangement that no longer works, and deep research or investigation. They are not chosen for gentle beginnings. Mula does, however, appear in the usual list of nakshatras accepted for marriage.

Questions

Is Mula nakshatra good?

Mula is neither good nor bad. It is a sharp, transforming star that gives depth, research ability and honesty. Its difficulty is restlessness and sudden change, which become strengths when the person commits to one field of study or service.

Which planet rules Mula nakshatra?

Ketu rules Mula. A person born with the Moon in Mula starts life in a Ketu mahadasha, which is 7 years long in full.

What are the Mula nakshatra name letters?

By the Avakahada chakra used for Hindu naming, the four padas give Ye, Yo, Bha and Bhi (ये, यो, भा, भी). Many families use the first syllable of the Moon’s pada for a baby’s name.

Which nakshatras are compatible with Mula?

Compatibility is judged by Ashtakoota between two Moon nakshatras, not by a fixed list. For Mula (Rakshasa gana, Adi nadi, dog yoni), a partner of the same gana scores best on gana, a partner whose nadi is not Adi avoids nadi dosha, and a friendly or same yoni scores on yoni. The full charts must be checked as well.

Which sign is Mula nakshatra in?

Mula lies entirely in Sagittarius, from 0°00' to 13°20' of the sign.
