Jyotish Concepts, Explained

The words every kundli uses — in plain language.

Lagna (Ascendant)

Changes roughly every 2 hours

Rashi (Moon Sign)

Changes every 2.25 days

Nakshatra (Birth Star)

27 nakshatras, 4 padas each = 108

Mahadasha

9 periods totalling 120 years

Sade Sati

Happens 2–3 times in a lifetime

Manglik (Mangal) Dosha

Present in roughly 40–50% of charts by the broad rule

Navamsa (D9)

Read with D1 for every prediction

Dashamsha (D10)

The career chart

Atmakaraka

Highest degree among 7 planets

Darakaraka

Lowest degree among 7 planets

Yogakaraka

Saturn for Taurus and Libra lagna

Exalted & Debilitated Planets

Each planet has one exaltation sign

Retrograde (Vakri) Planets

Sun and Moon are never retrograde

Gana (Deva, Manushya, Rakshasa)

9 nakshatras each

Ashtakoota (36 Guna Milan)

36 points in total

Rahu & Ketu

Always 180° apart
