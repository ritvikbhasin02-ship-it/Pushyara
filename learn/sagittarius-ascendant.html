Sagittarius Ascendant (Dhanu Lagna)

Ruled by Jupiter. Optimism, ethics, wisdom, adventure.

Sagittarius rising gives optimism, principle and a hunger for meaning. You teach, travel and expand; your growth comes from turning big visions into disciplined plans.
