Venus in the 3rd house: Artistic communication, design, media and creative skills.

What it means

Venus — Shukracharya, guru of desire — carries love, beauty, comfort, vehicles, art, marriage, luxury. The 3rd house (Sahaja Bhava) governs effort, siblings, skills, media, short travel, self-made initiative. Placed here, Venus's themes are expressed through this area of life: artistic communication, design, media and creative skills.

In career, love, money and health

  • Career: arts, fashion, luxury, hospitality, fragrance, film, design — coloured by the 3rd house themes of effort.
  • Relationships: works indirectly through courage.
  • Money: money follows effort in this area.
  • Health: watch kidneys, hormones, reproductive system.

Houses it aspects from here

From the 3rd house, Venus aspects your 9th house — fortune.

When it activates

The Venus mahadasha (20 years) and Venus sub-periods in other dashas bring this placement to the front. Transits of Jupiter and Saturn over this house trigger its events.
