Uttara Phalguni Nakshatra

Deity: Aryaman (god of contracts) · Ruler: Sun · Gana: Manushya

Uttara Phalguni nakshatra is the star of agreements: reliable, generous and principled, with a gift for friendship and lasting commitments.

What Uttara Phalguni nakshatra means

Uttara means “the latter”, so this is the second of the two Phalguni stars and the back legs of the bed. Its deity is Aryaman, an Aditya who presides over contracts, hospitality, patronage and the bonds of marriage and friendship. Where Bhaga gives the enjoyment, Aryaman gives the promise that makes it last.

The back legs of a bed stand for support and stability, the part that bears the weight. Uttara Phalguni natives are often exactly that for others. The Sun rules the nakshatra and adds dignity and a sense of duty.

Aryaman is named in the Vedic wedding hymns as the god who joins bride and groom and keeps the marriage bond. That is why Uttara Phalguni is so often linked with marriage, partnership and the keeping of promises.

Personality

Strengths are reliability, generosity, a strong sense of fairness and the ability to keep their word. They make loyal friends and good mediators, and they like to help, often quietly. People trust them with money, secrets and responsibility.

The shadow is dependence on approval. Because they care about reputation and helping, they can overextend, say yes too often and feel hurt when kindness is not noticed. The growth task is to give without keeping score.

In daily life this shows as the friend who remembers birthdays, returns what they borrow and mediates quarrels without taking sides. They are happiest when their help is valued, and they do best in steady routines where their reliability counts.

Career and money

The nakshatra suits work built on trust and agreement: management, human resources, administration, government service, law, diplomacy, mediation, social work, teaching, partnerships and hospitality. Contracts, brokerage and advisory roles also fit Aryaman's field. Careers tend to rise steadily and through relationships.

Love and marriage

Marriage is treated almost as a sacred contract here. Uttara Phalguni is committed, stable and loyal, and it expects the same. It prefers a friend and equal over drama.

For matching, the Ashtakoota method looks at gana and yoni among other points. Uttara Phalguni is Manushya gana, and same-gana pairs score best. Its yoni is the male cow, shared with Uttara Bhadrapada, and the classical enemy of the cow is the tiger. Nadi is Adi, and traditional matching prefers partners with a different nadi. These are only starting points.

Health

Classical lists link Virgo with digestion and the intestines, and Leo with the heart and back. Some lists give Uttara Phalguni the left hand and the generative region. Sources differ, so read this as symbolism, not a diagnosis.

The core of the nakshatra stays the same in all four padas. The navamsa sign and its lord change the flavour.

Pada 1, Sagittarius navamsa (Te). Jupiter rules this navamsa, and the first pada falls in Leo. It is the most principled and teaching-minded quarter, with an interest in dharma and wisdom.

Pada 2, Capricorn navamsa (To). Saturn adds discipline. This pada is career-focused, patient and steady, and suits slow, serious building.

Pada 3, Aquarius navamsa (Pa). Saturn again, with an Aquarian tone. It is more humanitarian and social, interested in groups, causes and service.

Pada 4, Pisces navamsa (Pi). Jupiter returns, with Pisces softness. This pada is kind, intuitive and spiritual, and is the most giving of the four.

Moon, lagna or a planet in Uttara Phalguni

Moon in Uttara Phalguni. This is your janma nakshatra. The Vimshottari sequence begins with the Sun, whose mahadasha lasts 6 years in all, the shortest; how much remains at birth depends on the Moon's degree. Such a person needs to feel useful and trusted to feel settled.

Uttara Phalguni rising. The nakshatra spans the end of Leo and the start of Virgo, so the ascendant can be Leo or Virgo. Either way it gives a helpful, dependable manner. Check the Sun and the sign lord.

Other planets in Uttara Phalguni. A planet here picks up themes of contracts, loyalty, service and long commitments. The Sun's authority steadies whatever it touches.

Good activities on Uttara Phalguni days

Uttara Phalguni is a dhruva (fixed) nakshatra in muhurta lists. It suits permanent undertakings: laying foundations, building a house, starting a steady job, agriculture and the sacred thread ceremony. Many lists also count it among the nakshatras for marriage. It is a poor fit for quick, short-term starts. Practical use: these days suit signing agreements, starting a long partnership, naming ceremonies and any undertaking meant to last for years. Avoid them for something you want to be quick and temporary.

Questions

Is Uttara Phalguni nakshatra good?

It is regarded as one of the steadier, kinder nakshatras, strong for friendship, marriage and responsibility. The thing to watch is needing approval. The whole chart decides.

Which planet rules Uttara Phalguni nakshatra?

The Sun rules Uttara Phalguni, so life begins in the Sun mahadasha, which runs 6 years in all.

What are the Uttara Phalguni nakshatra name letters?

The syllables are Te, To, Pa and Pi for padas 1 to 4.

Which nakshatras are compatible with Uttara Phalguni?

In Ashtakoota, Manushya gana partners score best with Uttara Phalguni, and Uttara Bhadrapada shares its cow yoni. Real compatibility needs both full charts.

Which sign is Uttara Phalguni in?

It spans two signs: pada 1 is in Leo and padas 2 to 4 are in Virgo, from 26°39' Leo to 9°59' Virgo.
