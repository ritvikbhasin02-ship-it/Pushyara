Shatabhisha Nakshatra

Deity: Varuna (cosmic waters, law) · Ruler: Rahu · Gana: Rakshasa

Shatabhisha nakshatra, ruled by Rahu and guarded by Varuna, is the star of the hundred healers: independent, private minds who solve what others cannot.

What Shatabhisha nakshatra means

Shatabhisha means hundred physicians, from shata for hundred and bhishaj for healer. The symbol is an empty circle, read as a hundred flowers, stars or healing herbs, and the open circle also suggests space and the void. The star lies wholly in Aquarius.

The deity is Varuna, the Vedic lord of the cosmic waters and of rta, the moral order, who sees all that is done in secret. Healing and truth are joined in him. Rahu rules the star, which gives it an unusual, scientific and sometimes foreign flavour.

Personality

Shatabhisha natives are independent, original and private. They see what is hidden, speak the truth even when it is unwelcome and are drawn to healing, science and unconventional ideas. Many have a calm surface and a very active inner life.

In real life: personality

For example, Abhishek, 28, a lab technician in Pune works nights and prefers a closed door. He spots patterns in test results before others, and shares them only with one close friend.

The shadow is isolation and secrecy. They may keep too much to themselves, turn critical when they feel misunderstood or swing between warmth and withdrawal. Letting a few people in is the lesson of the empty circle.

In daily life this star shows as a person who works best with a door they can close. They spot patterns before others, are often drawn to remedies, cures and systems, and may keep an odd schedule. The practical task is to share results and to have one or two people with whom they can speak freely, so that insight does not turn into isolation. Classical practice for the star is simple worship of its deity, Varuna, the keeper of cosmic order, kept neutral and free of fear.

Career and money

Medicine and pharmacology, alternative healing, research and laboratory work, technology and electronics, astronomy and astrology, aerospace, psychology and investigation all fit. Work with water, chemicals or electricity is also classical for the star.

In real life: work and money

Think of Dr Nirmala, a researcher in Chennai running a small herbal-testing lab with two colleagues. Her best work happens in a quiet room, and she earns from a few unusual consulting projects.

Rahu favours unusual paths, foreign links and later success. These natives often do best working alone or in a small expert team. Income can come in unusual forms, and steady habits help to balance it.

A useful way to read this star in a chart is to look at what the person studies in private. Shatabhisha natives often hold specialised knowledge that others value later, and the periods of Rahu and of Saturn, lord of Aquarius, tend to bring it into view. Patience with that timing matters more than pushing for early recognition, and a written record of their findings helps others see the value of the work.

Love and marriage

Shatabhisha is private and self-contained and needs space and loyalty. They are slow to open up, and once trust is built they commit deeply. A partner who respects their quiet time and does not pry suits them best.

In real life: love

Like Samar, who needs his own corner and long silences even in a happy marriage. A partner who does not take this personally and has one honest talk a week is what keeps him warm.

In Ashtakoota matching Shatabhisha is Rakshasa gana, horse yoni and Adi nadi. A partner who also has Adi nadi creates nadi dosha, so a Madhya or Antya partner is preferred. The classical opposite of horse yoni is buffalo yoni. Full matching needs both charts.

Health

Aquarius governs the calves and ankles. Lists for Shatabhisha itself differ, some giving the jaw and chin and others the lower legs. This is symbolic, not a medical statement.

Pada 1 (Go), Sagittarius navamsa, ruled by Jupiter: philosophical and optimistic, a teacher or healer with wide interests.

Pada 2 (Sa), Capricorn navamsa, ruled by Saturn: disciplined, practical and scientific. Ambition is strong and success is slow.

Pada 3 (Si), Aquarius navamsa, ruled by Saturn: the most unconventional pada. It is inventive, humanitarian and detached.

Pada 4 (Su), Pisces navamsa, ruled by Jupiter: compassionate, intuitive and mystical. Sensitivity is high.

Moon, lagna or a planet in Shatabhisha

Moon in Shatabhisha (janma nakshatra): a private, probing and restless mind. The first mahadasha is Rahu, which lasts 18 years in full. A long opening period like this tends to bring unusual early experiences, with the balance at birth set by the Moon's degree.

Lagna in Shatabhisha: a reserved, original manner and a pull towards study and healing. Saturn, lord of Aquarius, and the lagna lord show how this is used.

Another planet in Shatabhisha: it works in a hidden, researching way. Results often come through study, solitude or a break from convention.

Good activities on Shatabhisha days

Classical muhurta places Shatabhisha in the Chara (movable) group with Punarvasu, Swati, Shravana and Dhanishta. Movable days suit travel, buying a vehicle and starting a course of medical or scientific study. Shatabhisha also falls in the panchaka period, which many traditions avoid for roofing a house or travelling south.

Questions

Is Shatabhisha nakshatra good?

Shatabhisha is a powerful and unusual star. It gives healing ability, independence and deep thinking. Isolation and secrecy are its tests.

Which planet rules Shatabhisha nakshatra?

Rahu rules Shatabhisha. A person born with the Moon here begins life in a Rahu mahadasha, which is 18 years long in full.

What are the Shatabhisha nakshatra name letters?

The four padas give Go, Sa, Si and Su.

Which nakshatras are compatible with Shatabhisha?

Compatibility is judged by Ashtakoota between two Moon nakshatras, not by a fixed list. For Shatabhisha (Rakshasa gana, Adi nadi, horse yoni), a Rakshasa partner scores best on gana, a partner who is not Adi nadi avoids nadi dosha, and the full charts must be checked as well.

What is the symbol of Shatabhisha nakshatra?

An empty circle, read as a hundred flowers, stars or healing herbs. It stands for the hundred healers and for open space.
