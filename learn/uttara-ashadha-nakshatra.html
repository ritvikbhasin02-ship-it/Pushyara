Uttara Ashadha Nakshatra

Deity: Vishwadevas (universal gods) · Ruler: Sun · Gana: Manushya

Uttara Ashadha nakshatra, ruled by the Sun, is the star of final and lasting victory, won patiently and by ethical means.

What Uttara Ashadha nakshatra means

Uttara means later and Ashadha means unconquered, so the name is the later invincible one, the partner of Purva Ashadha. Where the first Ashadha wins by conviction, this one wins by endurance. The star spans the end of Sagittarius and the start of Capricorn, so its first pada is in Sagittarius and the other three are in Capricorn.

The deity is the Vishvedevas, the company of all gods invoked together in Vedic ritual, often listed as ten. They stand for universal law and shared duty. The symbol is an elephant's tusk, strength held in restraint, and some lists give the planks of a bed, rest after effort. The Sun rules the star and gives authority and a clear sense of self.

Personality

Uttara Ashadha natives are principled, patient and responsible. They think long term, keep their word and earn respect slowly. Others turn to them in a crisis because they are steady and fair, and they win through endurance and not display.

In real life: personality

For example, Mr Pillai, 55, in Thiruvananthapuram keeps tidy files of every receipt, even from 2009. Neighbours call him when a society matter must be done properly.

The shadow is rigidity and the loneliness of duty. They may carry too much, find it hard to ask for help, hold others to their own strict standard or put work before warmth. Learning to rest, which the bed symbol hints at, is part of the lesson.

In daily life this star shows as the person others call when something must be done properly. They finish what they begin, keep records and honour agreements, even small ones. The practical task is to share the load and to celebrate small wins along the way, since the big result may be years off. The tusk of the elephant is strong because it is not used carelessly. Classical practice for the star is simple worship of its deities, the Vishvedevas, who stand for the shared order of all gods.

Career and money

Government and administration, civil and armed services, law and the judiciary, large institutions, management, research, engineering and construction all suit this star. So do roles of trusted leadership such as principal, director or trustee. The fixed nature favours long careers inside one organisation or one field.

In real life: work and money

Think of Chitra, a civil engineer in a state PWD office in Raipur who spent 18 years on bridge projects. Recognition arrived later than for her peers, and has lasted.

Recognition often comes later than it does for peers, but it lasts. These natives are good at building something that outlives them: an institution, a family business or a body of work. The Sun's rule favours positions of authority more than quick trading.

Love and marriage

Uttara Ashadha is loyal, principled and serious about commitment. Marriage is a long contract for them, and they choose slowly. They may seem reserved at first and show love through reliability and support rather than words.

In real life: love

Like Anil, who proposed after four years of friendship and a clear plan for a home. He keeps small promises and expects the same in return, and is learning to rest on weekends.

In Ashtakoota matching this star is Manushya gana, mongoose yoni and Antya nadi. Manushya partners score well on gana, and a partner of a different nadi avoids nadi dosha. The classical opposite of mongoose yoni is serpent yoni. Full matching rests on all eight factors and both charts.

Health

Sagittarius governs the thighs and Capricorn the knees. Uttara Ashadha is commonly linked with the thighs, hips or waist, though lists differ. Read this as symbolism only.

Pada 1 (Bhe), Sagittarius navamsa, ruled by Jupiter: idealistic, philosophical and generous, a natural teacher. Watch for preaching.

Pada 2 (Bho), Capricorn navamsa, ruled by Saturn: the most disciplined and ambitious pada. The rise is slow and the career focus strong.

Pada 3 (Ja), Aquarius navamsa, ruled by Saturn: a reformer with social and humanitarian concerns. Ideas can be detached and ahead of their time.

Pada 4 (Ji), Pisces navamsa, ruled by Jupiter: compassionate, intuitive and spiritual. This pada may give up worldly gain for service.

Moon, lagna or a planet in Uttara Ashadha

Moon in Uttara Ashadha (janma nakshatra): a calm, dutiful mind that feels secure when it has a clear role and is doing right. The first mahadasha is the Sun, which lasts 6 years in full, the shortest of the nine, so themes of father, authority and self-worth come early. The balance at birth depends on the Moon's degree.

Lagna in Uttara Ashadha: a dignified, steady bearing and a pull towards leadership roles. The lagna lord matters more than the star itself here.

Another planet in Uttara Ashadha: it gains stamina and a sense of duty, and its results come slowly but hold. In the Capricorn part of the star planets turn more practical.

Good activities on Uttara Ashadha days

Classical texts place Uttara Ashadha in the Dhruva (fixed) group with Rohini, Uttara Phalguni and Uttara Bhadrapada. Fixed days suit things meant to last: laying a foundation, house-entry, planting trees, taking a permanent post and starting a long study or saving plan. The star also appears in the usual list of nakshatras accepted for marriage.

Questions

Is Uttara Ashadha nakshatra good?

Uttara Ashadha is widely regarded as a fortunate, stable star. It gives patience, ethics and lasting results. Its test is rigidity and carrying too much alone.

Which planet rules Uttara Ashadha nakshatra?

The Sun rules Uttara Ashadha. A person born with the Moon here begins life in a Sun mahadasha, which is 6 years long in full.

What are the Uttara Ashadha nakshatra name letters?

By the Avakahada chakra used for Hindu naming, the four padas give Bhe, Bho, Ja and Ji (भे, भो, जा, जी). Many families use the first syllable of the Moon’s pada for a baby’s name.

Which nakshatras are compatible with Uttara Ashadha?

Compatibility is judged by Ashtakoota between two Moon nakshatras, not by a fixed list. For Uttara Ashadha (Manushya gana, Antya nadi, mongoose yoni), a Manushya partner scores well on gana, a different nadi avoids nadi dosha, and the full charts must be checked as well.

Which signs does Uttara Ashadha nakshatra cover?

It begins at 26°40' Sagittarius and ends at 10° Capricorn. The first pada is in Sagittarius and the other three are in Capricorn.
