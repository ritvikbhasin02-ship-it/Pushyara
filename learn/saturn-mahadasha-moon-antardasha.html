Saturn Mahadasha – Moon Antardasha

About 1 years 7 months

Saturn–Moon lasts about 1 years 7 months. The Saturn mahadasha sets the stage — discipline, structure, responsibility and earned results — and the Moon sub-period brings in emotional peace, popularity, nourishment, mother's support, intuition.

What it typically brings

  • From Saturn: discipline, endurance, longevity, mass support, lasting success.
  • From Moon: emotional peace, popularity, nourishment, mother's support, intuition.
  • Watch: anxiety, mood swings, sleep problems, emotional dependence.

It depends on your chart

The results of Saturn–Moon depend on which houses Saturn and Moon rule and occupy for your ascendant, their strength, and the transits running at the time. Two people in the same sub-period can have opposite years.

What to do

Protect sleep and emotional hygiene: journal nightly, call your mother weekly, stay hydrated.
