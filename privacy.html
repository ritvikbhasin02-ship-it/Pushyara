Privacy policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

Pushyara is run by Ritvik Bhasin (sole proprietor), Satna, Madhya Pradesh, India. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

The name, date, time and place of birth you enter to make a report; your email and payment confirmation when you buy; and the notes you choose to save, such as marks in your prediction tracker.

When you order

The mobile number, email and shipping address you send with an order are used only to send your payment link, prepare your report and deliver your printed copy. Order details reach us by email through FormSubmit, a form-delivery service.

Unfinished orders and WhatsApp

If you type your mobile number or email on the order page and leave before finishing, we keep what you typed so we can remind you once about your order. If you ask for weekly good days or tick the offers box, we message you on WhatsApp; reply STOP at any time and we will stop. We never share your number.

How we use it

Only to make and show your report, keep your purchases, and answer you. We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

Payments

Payments are handled by our payment provider. We never see or store your card or UPI details.

Storage and deletion

Your report is saved in your own browser. You can delete any report from your device at any time, or write to hello@pushyara.com to have any data we hold deleted.

Children

The service is meant for adults. A parent may make a report for their child.

Changes

We will update this page if anything changes.
