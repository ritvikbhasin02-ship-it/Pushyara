Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

Pushyara is run by Ritvik Bhasin (sole proprietor), Satna, Madhya Pradesh, India.

Digital Life Report (₹499, one-time)

The digital report is delivered within 24 hours of payment. Because it is a file made for one person and sent to you, it cannot be returned once it has been delivered, so it is not refundable after delivery.

Two things are covered instead, and both are free:

  • Wrong birth details. If the date, time or place used to build your chart is not the one you gave us, write to hello@pushyara.com within 7 days and we will recalculate the whole report and send it again, at no cost.
  • One question answered. If something in your report is unclear, write once within 30 days and we will explain it in plain words, at no cost.

If your payment went through but no report arrived, that is a failed delivery, not a refund question. Write to us and we will either send the report or return your money in full.

Before you buy, you can read 13 real pages of a finished report in English or Hindi, and make a free kundli on the site, so you know exactly what you are paying for.

Printed editions (₹999, ₹1,499 and ₹2,499)

Each printed copy is made for one person, so it cannot be returned once printed. You can cancel for a full refund until we confirm it has gone to print. If your copy arrives damaged or with a printing fault, send a photo to hello@pushyara.com within 7 days of delivery and we will reprint and send it again at no cost.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.
