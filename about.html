Our Philosophy — Jyotish as a Decision System

Why Pushyara exists

Jyotish was never meant to frighten people. It is the science of time — kaala — and its purpose is to help a person act at the right moment, in the right direction, for the four aims of life.

The four purusharthas

Dharma — Purpose — who you are and what you came to do.

Artha — Prosperity — career, wealth, property and power.

Kama — Love — desire, marriage, children and joy.

Moksha — Freedom — past life, soul and liberation.

Every report we make is organised around these four aims, because a chart is not a verdict. It is a plan.

What we believe

  • Predict first, then advise. Every prediction names its planet, house and period. Every piece of advice is something you can actually do.
  • Dates, not drama. "Good times ahead" helps no one. "Your strongest career window is March 2028 to January 2029" does.
  • No fear-selling. No gemstones prescribed to everyone, no expensive pujas, no threats about doshas. Remedies are practical and measurable.
  • The whole life. A human life is not only career and marriage. Your report starts with purpose and what you are here to learn, and ends with the Gita verses for the road you are on.
  • Honesty. The astronomy is exact. The interpretation is traditional. Your free will remains the strongest planet in your chart.

Why "Pushyara"?

Pushya is the most auspicious of the 27 nakshatras: the star of nourishment, guided by Brihaspati, the teacher of the gods. Our mark is the Moon holding Pushya's three stars, the mind held steady by wisdom. That is what a good reading should do for you.
