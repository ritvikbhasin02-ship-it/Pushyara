      What is kundli matching?

      Kundli matching (kundli milan, gun milan or horoscope matching) is the traditional check families make before a marriage. It compares the birth charts, or kundlis, of the two people. The method this page uses is Ashtakoota milan: eight categories, called kootas, each worth a fixed number of points, adding up to 36. One point is a guna, which is why a result is spoken as “twenty-four gun”.

      Only the Moon is used. For each person the system takes the Moon sign (rashi) and the nakshatra, the one of 27 lunar mansions the Moon was crossing at birth, because Vedic astrology reads the Moon as the mind: temperament, mood, and how someone responds to people close to them. From those two facts it works out the eight kootas:

      • Varna (1) and Vashya (2): how the two handle ego and who gives way.
      • Tara (3): whether the pairing supports or drains each person’s health and luck.
      • Yoni (4): physical and instinctive ease.
      • Graha Maitri (5): mental friendship between the two minds.
      • Gana (6): temperament, whether gentle, practical or intense.
      • Bhakoot (7): the long arc of money, health and family.
      • Nadi (8): body constitution and the health of children.

      What does 18 out of 36 mean?

      The traditional minimum is 18, exactly half. Below 18 is called weak, 18 to 24 acceptable, 25 to 32 good and above 32 very good. These bands are convention, and two pandits may use slightly different ones. The tool shows the band and the line that goes with it, but the number deserves a closer look than the band does.

      An 18 does not mean the marriage has an even chance. It means that when you compare the two Moons across eight categories, half the possible points were scored. That says which areas to look at, not how life will go.

      Why a low score is not a veto

      Most of the 36 points sit in a few kootas. Nadi (8) and Bhakoot (7) together are 15 points. About one random couple in three shares a Nadi, and half of all random pairs of Moon signs fall into a flagged Bhakoot relation, so a zero in either is quite ordinary. Both have classical cancellations, which this tool lists in the “Doshas and cancellations” box and does not add to the score.

      A low total can also come from several small losses in Varna, Vashya and Yoni, which describe temperament and instinct and are far less weighty than the headline number suggests. So read the table row by row. A couple on 16 with every loss in the small kootas looks very different from a couple on 16 with a Nadi zero and a Bhakoot zero. We would not turn down a match on the score alone, and we would not ignore a low one.

      What matters beyond the 36 points

      Guna milan is one layer. The layers that usually decide more are in the full charts:

      • The Navamsa (D9) chart. The nine-part chart classically used to read the strength of marriage in each life.
      • The 7th house and its lord. The part of each chart that describes partnership, with Venus as a key planet.
      • Dasha overlap. The dasha is the planetary period each person is in. Two people in easy periods at the same time meet life differently from two in heavy ones, and the periods change on known dates.
      • Manglik status. Mars in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th or 12th house. The box above shows both. A Manglik with a Manglik is traditionally considered to cancel out.

      Beyond the chart, the things that carry a marriage are ordinary: honesty, how each handles anger and money, how the two families get on, and whether both want the same life. No kundli measures these, and none can promise an outcome.

      Questions

      Is this kundli matching free?

      Yes. The score, all eight kootas, the dosha notes and the Manglik check are free, with no sign-up. Your details are only sent to us if you ask for weekly good days on WhatsApp.

      How many gunas are needed for marriage?

      The traditional minimum is 18 out of 36. 18 to 24 is usually called acceptable and 25 and above good. It is a convention and the full charts matter more.

      What if I do not know the exact birth time?

      The Moon sign and nakshatra, which guna milan uses, are usually right even if the time is an hour or two off, unless the Moon was close to a sign or nakshatra boundary. The Manglik check from the ascendant needs a closer time.

      Which method does this tool use?

      Ashtakoota milan with eight kootas and 36 points, calculated from the sidereal (Lahiri) Moon of each person. Nadi and Bhakoot cancellations are shown in a separate notes box and are not added to the score.

      Why is my score different on another website?

      The framework is shared, but books differ on small details such as half points in Vashya and Graha Maitri, and some sites apply cancellations to the total. A difference of a point or two is normal.

      Is a low score a reason to refuse a match?

      Not by itself. Check which koota lost points, whether a cancellation applies, and then read the Navamsa chart, the 7th house and the dasha periods of both people.
